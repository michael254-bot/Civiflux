About Us

CiviFlux Engineering Ltd (CFEL). incorporated in Kenya in 2019, is an independent Preconstruction , Construction and Post-construction Quality Control Company servicing a broad range of clients. The company’s operations are based in Nairobi, Kenya’s capital but serves clients across the country and beyond. We believe that independent third party testing is a cost effective way to protect and limit liability for all parties involved in Construction, Manufacturing, Property Acquisition or production process. CFEL is a Company having a team of qualified and dedicated experienced young professionals who have developed special skill in the use of incorporating computer aided technologies in the field of Planning and Management, Architectural & Structural design and Construction Works as well.

As an independent Quality Control Company, CFEL carries out its operations as per ISO/IEC 17025:2017

Our team of experienced engineers combines technical expertise with modern testing methods to ensure every project meets regulatory standards and client expectations. From concept and design to construction and inspection, we are driven by precision, integrity, and excellence.

Our Mission: To provide reliable engineering solutions that ensure safety, durability, and value in every structure we deliver.

Our Services

1. Structural Audits, Destructive and Non-Destructive Tests

Our engineers perform a structural audit, identifying any cracks, weak beams, or deterioration. We provide a detailed report and recommendations to prevent future failures

2. Construction Material Analysis

We test aggregates, asphalt, and concrete for strength and durability, ensuring the project is built with reliable materials that last longer.

3. Geotechnical Investigations

We determine the type of foundation required and assess risks like soil settlement or landslides, helping developers plan safe and cost-effective foundations.

4. In-Situ Tests for Roads and Buildings

we carry out on-site tests, like soil compaction for roads and concrete strength checks for the building. This verifies that work is being done correctly and meets project specifications.

5. Design, construction and Quality Control

our engineers create detailed designs and blueprints. During construction, we monitor materials, workmanship, and processes to ensure the building is safe, functional, and meets regulations.
